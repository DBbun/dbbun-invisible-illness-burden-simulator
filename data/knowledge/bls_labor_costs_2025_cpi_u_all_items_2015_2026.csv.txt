ECONOMIC USE
The latest usable annual index in this retained file is 2025. Convert a documented origin-year dollar amount with target CPI/origin CPI. This changes purchasing-power units, not disease burden, insurance coverage or actual patient payment. Do not adjust an amount twice if its paper already reports target-year dollars.

DECISIONS BY AUDIENCE
Patients and caregivers: Check the price year before comparing published dollar amounts.
Care delivery: Put pathway unit costs in one stated price year before comparing resource use.
Payers and policy: Use CPI-U for general purchasing-power comparisons; justify a medical-specific or wage-specific alternative when that is the decision question.

MISSING EVIDENCE / NEXT ANALYSIS
No original cost amount is supplied; no hypothetical cost was inserted.
No forecast of future inflation or disease-specific medical price index.

DO NOT USE FOR
Treating partial-year averages as complete annual observations, or using general inflation as evidence of increased illness incidence.

Analysis is source-backed but has not had independent scientific review. No modeled savings or assumed effects are supplied.
