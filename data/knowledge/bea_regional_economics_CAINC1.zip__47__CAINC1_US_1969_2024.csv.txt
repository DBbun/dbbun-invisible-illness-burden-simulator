ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
Personal income, per-capita income and population have different denominators. Per-capita income is not an hourly wage and may include transfers and nonlabor income. It can contextualize affordability only alongside an independently measured household cost burden.

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
Use the exact geography and 1969–2024 coverage in this file for context, not a live estimate. State-specific and ALL_AREAS members overlap; retrieving both must not double their economic totals. Ranges describe available rows and exclude nonnumeric disclosure/availability markers, not zero-valued observations.
