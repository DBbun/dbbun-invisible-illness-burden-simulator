FINDING AND ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
This file contains 13,180 aggregate cells spanning 1992–2007. It supports reported-case workload and coverage analysis. A confirmed/probable surveillance case is not the same denominator as a diagnosed-and-treated insurance episode. It supplies no treatment cost, diagnostic delay, navigation uptake or preventable fraction. A growing count cannot by itself justify a growing causal cost estimate.

COMPARABILITY DECISION
Keep this file paired with its era-specific definition. The 2022 surveillance definition/reporting break must remain visible. The geographic and non-geographic releases overlap; choose one counting representation for each analysis. Do not add their totals or infer suppressed categories from differences.

DECISIONS BY AUDIENCE
Patients and caregivers: Use as community-level context, not a diagnosis or estimate of your own costs.
Care delivery: Check whether the catchment state and year are represented before using a reported-case workload benchmark.
Payers and policy: Use reported surveillance counts only with an explicitly matched cost population; obtain a justified transport model before applying claims episode costs.
