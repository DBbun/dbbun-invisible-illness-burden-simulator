ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
The current-symptom and activity-limitation measures support different planning questions: scale of current need versus functional intensity. This file supplies no hourly wage, lost hours, caregiver burden or support-program effect. Estimates are self-reported survey quantities and overlap the retained Census HPS microdata; they are not an independent population to add to HPS.

DECISIONS BY AUDIENCE

Care delivery: Use the appropriate population denominator for access and functional-support planning.
Payers and policy: Keep survey uncertainty attached to the selected period and indicator. Obtain independently supported per-person costs before monetization.
