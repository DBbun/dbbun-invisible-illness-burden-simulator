J0696 FINANCIAL SIGNAL
Between 2020 and 2024, retained gross spending moved from $1,487,511.69 to $1,074,671.75, claims from 624,122 to 576,627, and computed spending per claim from $2.3834 to $1.8637. These components should be examined separately: a lower per-claim amount can coexist with greater service volume. No part of the change is attributed here to Lyme, treatment effectiveness or a price intervention.

SCOPE AND OVERLAP
The complete drug file is preserved, but this analysis deliberately filters the named antibiotic ingredients; other drugs are not analytically interpreted. A drug can be used for multiple indications. Part D gross expenditures include payer and beneficiary components and exclude undisclosed rebates. Medicaid aggregates are not a household cash price. Ceftriaxone J0696 is a 250 mg billing unit; no dose or course length is assumed.

DECISIONS BY AUDIENCE
Patients and caregivers: Ask your insurer/pharmacist for your own covered payment; the national average per claim is not your bill.
Care delivery: Use the exact product, route and billing unit for costing an already justified care pathway; do not choose treatment from spending data.
Payers and policy: Compare spending, volume and per-claim changes separately. Apply a condition-attribution fraction only if supported by a separate compatible study.

MISSING EVIDENCE / NEXT ANALYSIS
