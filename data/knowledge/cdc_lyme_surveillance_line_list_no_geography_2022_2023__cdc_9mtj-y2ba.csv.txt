FINDING AND ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
This file contains 152,018 reported-case rows spanning 2022–2023. It supports reported-case workload and coverage analysis. A confirmed/probable surveillance case is not the same denominator as a diagnosed-and-treated insurance episode. It supplies no treatment cost, diagnostic delay, navigation uptake or preventable fraction. A growing count cannot by itself justify a growing causal cost estimate.

COMPARABILITY DECISION
Keep this file paired with its era-specific definition. The 2022 surveillance definition/reporting break must remain visible. The geographic and non-geographic releases overlap; choose one counting representation for each analysis. Do not add their totals or infer suppressed categories from differences.
